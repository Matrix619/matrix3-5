The Standard

No shortcuts. No excuses.

Show up prepared. Respect the customer. Know the system. Communicate clearly. Own the result.

Why Matrix

Matrix is growing across residential, commercial, and industrial HVAC and mechanical service. That means the company needs people who can do more than complete a task. We need people who think, troubleshoot, communicate, and take pride in the finished work.

We are building systems around service, preventive maintenance, controls, equipment reliability, customer relationships, and field execution. As Matrix grows, the goal is to create real career paths for technicians, mechanics, controls professionals, field leaders, and operations staff who want to grow with the company.

You do not have to know everything on day one. You do need to care about learning, doing the work correctly, and being someone your team and the customer can depend on.

What We Expect

Know your work

Understand the equipment, use good troubleshooting habits, and ask questions when you do not know.

Communicate

Keep customers and the team informed. Clear communication prevents small problems from becoming bigger ones.

Take ownership

If you touch the job, care about the result. Do not walk past problems and assume someone else will handle them.

Respect the customer

Be professional, protect the property, explain the work, and leave the job better than you found it.

Keep learning

HVAC, controls, mechanical systems, and technology keep changing. We expect the team to keep developing.

Do it right

We would rather solve the real problem than rush through a call and create the next one.

What You Can Expect
  • Clear expectations and accountability
  • Respect for skilled trade work
  • A company that values organization and communication
  • Exposure to residential, commercial, industrial, controls, and maintenance work as opportunities grow
  • Room to take on more responsibility when you prove you are ready
  • A company focused on building long-term customer relationships, not just running calls
Be Straight With Yourself

Matrix may not be for everyone.

If you cut corners, avoid responsibility, do not communicate, or only do good work when someone is watching, this probably is not the right place.

If you take pride in your trade, want to improve, and want your work to mean something, we should talk.
