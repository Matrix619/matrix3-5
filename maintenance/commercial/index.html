Commercial & Industrial PM Programs

Maintenance built around the facility—not a residential template.

Matrix commercial and industrial agreements are structured around equipment, operating conditions, service frequency, and reliability requirements. Bronze, Silver, and Gold provide a clear starting point, while the final scope is built around the site.

Bronze

Annual PM

1 scheduled PM annually

Good for lower-use equipment or facilities that want a structured annual inspection and documented service relationship.

Silver

Seasonal PM

2 scheduled PM visits annually

Designed for spring/fall or cooling/heating seasonal service where consistent inspection and preparation matter.

Gold

Quarterly PM

4 scheduled PM visits annually

For facilities and equipment where uptime, reliability, and more frequent mechanical oversight matter.

Agreement Scope

Programs can be built around the actual mechanical load.

Commercial and industrial agreements can be structured around the factors that materially affect service requirements and operating risk.

Equipment count and type
Tonnage and operating hours
Single-site or multi-site coverage
Response-time requirements
Filters, belts, and planned materials
RTUs, split systems, chillers, boilers, and refrigeration
Controls and BAS support
Quarterly, seasonal, annual, or custom schedules
