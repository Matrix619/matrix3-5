Residential Maintenance Plans

Two seasonal visits. Three levels of member value.

Every Matrix residential plan includes a spring cooling PM and a fall heating PM. Bronze, Silver, and Gold are differentiated by priority, repair savings, service protection, and member benefits—not by adding unnecessary maintenance visits.

Bronze

Essential Care

Maintain my system.
  • Spring cooling PM
  • Fall heating PM
  • Matrix service history
  • Standard filter during PM*
  • 10% repair savings
  • Member service priority

Best for: Homeowners who want consistent preventive maintenance.

Silver

Priority Care

Maintenance plus stronger service protection.
  • Everything in Bronze
  • 15% repair savings
  • Faster priority scheduling
  • Reduced diagnostic/service fees
  • Enhanced repair warranty
  • Reduced after-hours benefit

Best for: Homeowners who want maintenance plus stronger service protection.

Gold

Premier Care

Matrix's highest level of residential service.
  • Everything in Silver
  • 20% repair savings
  • Highest scheduling priority
  • Strongest after-hours benefit
  • Annual replacement loyalty credit
  • Maximum repair warranty benefit

Best for: Homeowners who want Matrix's highest level of residential service.

*Standard filter benefit applies to qualifying standard filters. Specialty, high-efficiency, and non-standard filters are priced separately. Plan terms, limits, response benefits, warranties, and loyalty credits are defined in the applicable Matrix maintenance agreement.
